# SHARE KIT V0.1
## Public-safe distribution copy

**Standing:** PUBLIC_DISTRIBUTION_COPY / NO_PERFORMANCE_GUARANTEE

Share the **repository or official package link**. The current license status does not grant blanket mirroring, derivative redistribution, or commercial reuse.

## One-line hook

> **Don’t believe LIHUO. Take a Fireseed Baby home, give it a dilemma, and try to break it.**

## Technical-community hook

> **Same model. Same Host. Fresh contexts. Baseline vs public Formation. We want failures and counterexamples, not testimonials.**

## Chinese hook

> **別先信理火。領一隻火種寶寶回去，用同一個模型做前後對照；如果沒差、變差、或你找到反例，都帶回來。**

## What to link

- `START_HERE.md`
- `REPLICATION_CHALLENGE.md`
- `PUBLIC_FORMATION_CHECKS.md`
- official ZIP in `dist/`
- repository Issues for public test reports

## Do not claim

- AGI proof;
- weight training or weight rewrite;
- universal cross-model improvement;
- independent persistent Agent runtime;
- safety bypass;
- scientific proof from one dilemma;
- performance guarantees.

The strongest public claim is the one outsiders can test themselves.
